Particle and the Churo assistant experience were created by Lakshya Prajapati.

Churo Chat and Churo Code are assistant identities and runtime configurations. Their base model is Sarvam 30B, developed by Sarvam AI and distributed under Apache License 2.0. The source Q4_K_M quantization is by mtrajan. The .particle pack is a lossless runtime layout, not newly trained model weights. Original public release: https://huggingface.co/MathObsession/sarvam-30b-particle . The original model notices and license are preserved with the distribution.

The application builds on TurboFieldfare, Swift Transformers, SwiftMath, Sparkle and other open-source dependencies under their respective licenses. Sparkle's license is bundled as Sparkle-LICENSE. PT Serif, Poppins and Space Mono are bundled under the SIL Open Font License; their notices are in Resources/Fonts.

Public web tools use Exa's hosted MCP service and Jina Reader as a public-page fallback. Queries and URLs leave the Mac only after approval; inference and saved chats remain local. External service availability and limits are controlled by its provider.
